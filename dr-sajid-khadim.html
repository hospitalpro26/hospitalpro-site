About the doctor

Dr. Sajid Khadim — Pediatrician in Sukkur

Dr. Sajid Khadim (also listed as Dr. Sajid Khadim Ali Shar) is a pediatrician and Paediatrics Registrar at Dr. Ziauddin Hospital Sukkur. He holds an MBBS, FCPS in Paediatrics and PGPN Pro qualification. His profile describes more than nine years of experience across pediatric wards, newborn care, pediatric emergencies and hospital-based neonatal and pediatric intensive care.

His clinical background includes work at Dr. Ziauddin University Hospital Sukkur, Children's Hospital Sukkur and GMMMC Teaching Hospital, along with experience in neonatal resuscitation, newborn stabilization, respiratory support, and management of pediatric and neonatal infections.
