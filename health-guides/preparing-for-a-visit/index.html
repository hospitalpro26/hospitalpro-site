How to prepare for a medical appointment

A patient-friendly checklist for clinic visits, medical questions and appointment preparation.

Updated 31 October 2026 · General patient-navigation resource

Preparing a short summary of your concerns can help you communicate clearly during a medical appointment. You do not need to diagnose yourself before seeking care.

Note your main concern

Consider when your symptoms started, what has changed and what questions you want answered. If you have multiple concerns, it may help to identify your priorities for the appointment.

Bring relevant information

When available, bring previous prescriptions, current medication names and doses, known allergies, relevant test results and a summary of important medical history. Share these directly with your clinician rather than uploading them to a public directory or comment page.

Ask questions during the visit

It is reasonable to ask what a proposed test is for, whether follow-up is needed, how to take prescribed medication correctly and which signs should prompt more urgent attention. Tell your doctor when you do not understand a recommendation.

Confirm practical details

Check clinic location, OPD timing, appointment requirements and accessibility arrangements with the facility. If urgent symptoms occur before your planned visit, do not wait for a scheduled appointment.
