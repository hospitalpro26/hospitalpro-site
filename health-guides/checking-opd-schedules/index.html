How to confirm a doctor's OPD schedule

Avoid unnecessary clinic visits: what to verify before attending an OPD appointment.

Updated 31 October 2026 · General patient-navigation resource

An outpatient department (OPD) schedule describes published consultation days and times. It may change because of emergencies, surgery, holidays, staff leave or hospital operational needs. A schedule listed on an informational page should never be taken as guaranteed same-day availability.

Find the correct clinic

Some doctors consult at more than one hospital or in different cities. Check the precise clinic and city rather than relying on the doctor's name alone.

Confirm the appointment method

Review the doctor’s linked Slashcure page for updates and any available consultation options. If availability is not confirmed, use an official clinic telephone number before travelling. HospitalPro itself does not issue appointment confirmations or booking tokens.

Ask about arrival and documents

Where applicable, ask about registration procedures, appointment or token systems, relevant investigations and clinic payment arrangements. Do not assume a payment is required or that a particular service is available from an online listing.

Keep a fallback plan

For time-sensitive care, ask the facility about alternatives. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, seek immediate care through an emergency service.
