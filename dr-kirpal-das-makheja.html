Professional summary

Prof. Dr. Kirpal Das Makheja is a Professor of Medicine and Senior Consultant Physician with a clinical practice spanning general internal medicine, gastroenterology and hepatology. His stated expertise includes diagnostic upper gastrointestinal endoscopy, colonoscopy, and evidence-informed evaluation of stomach, intestinal and liver complaints.
