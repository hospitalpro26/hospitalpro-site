Common questions about doctors and hospitals

Answers about HospitalPro doctor listings, schedules, appointment links and how to suggest a website FAQ.

Are these live doctor appointment times?

No. HospitalPro publishes profile and scheduled OPD information. Actual availability must be checked on the connected Slashcure page or directly with the clinic.

Does HospitalPro recommend the best doctor?

No. Featured listings are informational and may be promotional; they are not independent clinical rankings, professional endorsements or outcome comparisons.

Can I book an appointment on HospitalPro?

HospitalPro itself does not confirm bookings. Open a healthcare professional’s Slashcure page for available options, which may differ by provider.

How do I update incorrect doctor details?

Contact us with the public URL and a verifiable correction. Do not include medical records.

How do I suggest a question for this page?

Use website feedback to propose a general question, or email slashcureofficial@gmail.com. Questions are manually reviewed before any public publication. We do not publish user-provided patient histories or personal health questions.
