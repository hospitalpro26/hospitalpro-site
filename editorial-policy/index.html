Editorial and listing policy

Read how HospitalPro maintains doctors, hospitals, health guides, corrections and sponsored listings.

Last reviewed: 31 October 2026. HospitalPro is an informational and promotional directory. Inclusion is not a medical recommendation, clinical quality rating, employment verification, endorsement or guarantee of appointment availability.

Our information sources

Doctor and hospital profiles are assembled from publicly available facility information, supplied professional profile details and published Slashcure listings. We aim to attribute current schedules to their original listings and avoid unsupported claims about clinical outcomes.

Schedule freshness

Clinic hours and hospital services may change without notice. We distinguish published schedules from live availability. To verify a visit, consult the linked Slashcure listing or contact the facility directly.

Health education

Our health guides provide general, non-individualized information. They do not replace assessment, diagnosis, treatment or emergency services. We do not label an article clinically reviewed unless such review has actually taken place. Substantive medical claims should have appropriate sources and dated review information.

Promotions and fairness

Featured placement may be promotional. Listings are not ranked by surgical success, competence or patient outcomes. There is no guarantee that a listed healthcare provider will be the right choice for any particular patient.

Corrections

Please report factual errors through our feedback page or email slashcureofficial@gmail.com. Explain the public listing involved, the correction requested and a suitable verification source. Do not send clinical records.

HospitalPro does not provide diagnoses, individualized treatment or emergency care. Check current doctor and hospital information directly with the healthcare provider.
