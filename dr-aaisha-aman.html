Meet the Specialist

Meet Dr. Aaisha Aman, Gynecologist in Sukkur.

Dr. Aaisha Aman is a Consultant Gynaecologist and Obstetrician, Laparoscopic Surgeon and Infertility Specialist practising in Sukkur. She is Assistant Professor and Section Head of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at Dr. Ziauddin Hospital, Sukkur Campus.

With over 10 years of clinical experience, her interests include high-risk pregnancy management, antenatal and postnatal care, infertility evaluation, gynaecological disorders, and minimally invasive laparoscopic procedures. She also contributes to medical teaching and training. She is also listed as Dr. Aisha Aman on some doctor directories.
