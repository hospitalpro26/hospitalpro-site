Cookie Policy

How HospitalPro uses cookies, local browser storage and third-party web services.

Effective date: 1 November 2026.

Cookies on the public directory

As of 1 November 2026, HospitalPro's core public directory pages do not intentionally set first-party advertising or analytics cookies and do not operate a first-party patient tracking system. We do not currently ask visitors to accept non-essential marketing cookies because no such cookie feature is enabled in the website code we maintain.

Browser-local demo data

The separate appointment demonstration is a prototype, not a real booking service. It uses browser localStorage to save its simulated token and reception data on the device running the demo. This is not a cookie, and the data is not shared with another device or sent to a HospitalPro booking server. Do not enter real patient names, phone numbers, health information or payment details in the demo. Clearing the website's local storage in your browser removes the saved demo data.

Hosting, fonts, images and external links

Our website host may process standard server requests and logs for delivery and security. Some pages request assets such as web fonts or images from external services, which may receive information such as your IP address and browser details. Clicking links to Slashcure, WhatsApp, social networks or other websites transfers you to services with their own cookies and privacy practices.

Analytics or cookie changes

If we introduce analytics, advertising pixels, third-party embeds or other non-essential cookie-based features, we will review and update this policy and provide consent controls where applicable before deploying them. This policy describes the current website implementation, not future integrations.

Your browser controls

You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Disabling storage may reset the appointment demo and may affect third-party websites you choose to visit.

Questions

For privacy or cookie enquiries, contact info@slashcure.com or read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

HospitalPro is a public healthcare information directory, not an emergency or clinical consultation service. For urgent medical concerns, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.
