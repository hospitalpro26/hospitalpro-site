About the Specialist

Experience focused on digestive and liver health

Dr. Murk Channa (MBBS, FCPS) is a Consultant Gastroenterologist and Hepatologist in Sukkur, Sindh, who also practices as a Consultant Physician. Her professional background covers gastroenterology, hepatology and internal medicine, with clinical experience at Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC), Karachi.

She evaluates and manages conditions affecting the stomach, intestines and liver, including hepatitis B and C, fatty liver, gastritis, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), peptic ulcers, chronic digestive symptoms and gastrointestinal bleeding.

Her profile describes experience with diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy. Care emphasizes careful assessment, evidence-based treatment and patient communication. Slashcure describes Dr. Murk Channa as Sukkur's first female Consultant Gastroenterologist and Hepatologist.
