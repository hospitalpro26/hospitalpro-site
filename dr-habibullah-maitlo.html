About Dr. Habibullah Maitlo

Dr. Habibullah Maitlo is an FCPS-qualified Consultant Paediatric Surgeon at Dr. Ziauddin Hospital, Sukkur Campus, and is also listed at Ali Medical Center in Khairpur. He provides surgical assessment and management for newborns, infants, children and adolescents, including selected congenital conditions, paediatric surgical emergencies, preoperative assessment and postoperative follow-up.

His published areas of care include hernia and hydrocele, appendicitis, bowel obstruction, congenital gastrointestinal concerns and paediatric urological conditions.
