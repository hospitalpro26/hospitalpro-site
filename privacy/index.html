Privacy and data handling

How HospitalPro presents public healthcare information, handles feedback and links to external services.

Last reviewed: 31 October 2026. HospitalPro is a public healthcare discovery website operated as a promotional companion to Slashcure. It does not provide patient accounts, clinical record storage or real-time appointment processing on this website.

Information visible on the website

HospitalPro publishes information about healthcare professionals and facilities, including public names, qualifications, specialties and published OPD details. Contact a listed provider directly to confirm updates or request corrections.

Feedback sent by email

Our feedback page opens a draft in your own email application addressed to slashcureofficial@gmail.com. The draft is not sent by HospitalPro; you choose whether to send it. If you send the email, the details you include will be processed through your email provider and the recipient's mailbox in order to review and respond to your feedback. Do not include patient names, records, symptoms or other sensitive health information.

Analytics and external services

Website hosting and external embedded images may involve network requests and server logs under their respective providers' policies. HospitalPro links to Slashcure and social-media websites; once you follow those links, their terms and privacy practices apply. The current HospitalPro pages do not use a first-party patient-tracking system.

Contact and corrections

For factual listing corrections, privacy requests or general enquiries, email slashcureofficial@gmail.com. For healthcare or urgent medical concerns, contact an appropriate doctor or emergency service instead.

HospitalPro does not provide diagnoses, individualized treatment or emergency care. Check current doctor and hospital information directly with the healthcare provider.
